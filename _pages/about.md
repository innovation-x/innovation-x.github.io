I am a Ph.D. candidate at [The University of Osaka](https://www.es.osaka-u.ac.jp/ja/), advised by Prof. Hiroshi Ishiguro and Prof. Yutaka Nakamura, and affiliated with [Ishiguro Lab](https://eng.irl.sys.es.osaka-u.ac.jp/). I also work as a [Junior Research Associate](https://www.riken.jp/en/careers/programs/jra/) in the [Behavior Learning Group](https://grp.riken.jp/labs/behav_learn/), Information R&D and Strategy Headquarters, RIKEN, in Kyoto, Japan. My research focuses on multimodal generative modeling for robotics, particularly world models, world-action models, multimodal sensor fusion, and cross-modal representation learning.

I aim to enable robots to better understand the world and anticipate human behavior in human–robot interaction. My work explores how multimodal observations can support future prediction, action understanding, and more natural robot behavior.

I received my M.Eng. from [The University of Osaka](https://www.es.osaka-u.ac.jp/ja/), where I graduated as an International Honors Student in the Department of Systems Innovation. I earned my B.Eng. in Mechatronic Engineering from Tongji University, advised by [Qirong Tang](http://robotics-tongji.org/).

I am from Shanghai, China, and have lived in Japan for about four years. Outside research, I enjoy fishing, traveling, and snowboarding—which makes winter my favorite season. :)

My experience includes:

* Multimodal data collection and data-processing pipelines
* Generative-model development, including Video Diffusion Transformers, world models, and world-action models
* Embedded and ROS/ROS 2-based robotic systems

**If you are interested in collaborating, please feel free to contact me! ^_^**
